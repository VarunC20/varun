About me

I am a Mechanical Engineer with a strong passion for Robotics. Over the years, I have worked on various robotics projects, including OSAKE, a self-learning robot 🤖 that continues to evolve as a practical embodiment of my research interests.

In my current role, I contribute to the research and development of automation tools at Oriental Yeast Co. Ltd (a Nisshin Seifun Group subsidiary). I am also committed to continuous learning and actively enhancing my project management skills to better lead and execute complex, multidisciplinary projects.

At the Institute of Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Tech), I am pursuing my Master’s degree with a focus on next-generation actuators and lightweight collaborative robots. My research includes advanced actuator development at the Yaskawa Future Technology Laboratory, as well as exploring reservoir computing at the University of Cambridge.

Beyond my professional and academic work, I create short documentaries on my YouTube channel, Explained (@explainedshow), where I simplify complex topics for a broader audience.

Let’s connect and explore the future of robotics and automation together.
